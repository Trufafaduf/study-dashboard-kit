# Mohs Hardness

The Mohs scale ranks minerals 1–10 by which scratches which (GEO 110 lecture 2):
talc 1, gypsum 2, calcite 3, fluorite 4, apatite 5, orthoclase 6, quartz 7,
topaz 8, corundum 9, diamond 10. A mineral scratches anything with a lower
number.

Field references: fingernail about 2.5, copper coin about 3.5, steel knife or
glass about 5.5.

**Trap.** The scale is ordinal. Diamond (10) is far more than twice as hard as
fluorite (5) in absolute terms; the numbers give order, not ratios.
