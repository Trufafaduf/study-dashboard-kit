# Plate Boundaries

Three kinds of margin (GEO 110 lecture 4):

- **Divergent**: plates move apart and new crust forms from rising magma
  (mid-ocean ridges, rift valleys).
- **Convergent**: plates move together. Where oceanic crust meets continental
  crust, the denser oceanic plate subducts, giving a trench and a volcanic arc.
  Two continents collide and build mountains instead.
- **Transform**: plates slide past each other; crust is neither made nor
  destroyed, and earthquakes are common.

**Trap.** Volcanoes at a convergent margin sit on the overriding plate, not the
one that sinks.
