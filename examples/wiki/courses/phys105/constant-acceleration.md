# Constant acceleration

Under constant acceleration $a$, with initial speed $u$:

- $v = u + at$
- $s = ut + 	frac12 at^2$
- $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$

Pick the equation that leaves out the quantity you neither know nor want.

**Worked example.** A car starts from rest and accelerates at $2\ 	ext{m/s}^2$ for $5\ 	ext{s}$: $s = 	frac12 \cdot 2 \cdot 25 = 25\ 	ext{m}$.

**Trap**: using $s = vt$ when the speed is changing.
