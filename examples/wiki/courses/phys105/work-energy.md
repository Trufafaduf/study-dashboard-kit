# Work and energy

The net work done on a body equals its change in kinetic energy, $W = \Delta(\tfrac12 mv^2)$. With no friction, $mgh$ lost equals $\tfrac12 mv^2$ gained.

**Worked example.** A ball dropped from 5 m: $v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.8 \cdot 5} \approx 9.9\ \text{m/s}$.

**Trap**: forgetting the square root.
