# Newton's second law

The net force on a body equals its mass times its acceleration, $F_{	ext{net}} = ma$. Draw every force, choose axes, sum each axis.

**Worked example.** A 2 kg block pulled by 10 N against 4 N of friction: $a = (10 - 4)/2 = 3\ 	ext{m/s}^2$.

**Trap**: using the applied force instead of the net force.
